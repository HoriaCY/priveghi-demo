Priveghi

A demo of a card table kept at a wake. The rules run in Rust compiled to WebAssembly; the table is drawn in SVG by code.

Type

Alegreya and Alegreya SC, copyright 2011 The Alegreya Project Authors, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (licence).

Cormorant Unicase, copyright 2015 the Cormorant Project Authors, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (licence).

The fonts are served from this site, subset to the letters Romanian needs, comma-below S and T included.

Pictures and sound

Every picture is drawn by code, from the motifs of Romanian folk craft. Every sound is synthesized in the browser. Nothing was bought.

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no requests to any other site. The wake is saved only in this browser's local storage, and nowhere else.
