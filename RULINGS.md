# Rulings

Judgment calls made while building, each with its source and the alternative. Every one is a
one-line change. **R1–R18 were ruled by the director on 2026-10-13**: accepted as written, except
R14 (changed), with R19–R23 added from the same answers. Later rows are provisional until ruled. Sources: the demo brief (`PRIVEGHI_DEMO_BRIEF.md`), the systems chapter
(`SYSTEMS_v2_1.md`), and the build rule that the chapter's numbers win where the two overlap.

R1–R47 are the watcher's demo's (the first fiction; its engine, tests and screenshots left at step 6 of the turnover, R139, and the rows stay for the record). R48 onward belong to the turnover to the dead's wake (`SYSTEMS_v3_MORTUL.md`, the addendum v3), M0b T1, one build step at a time; the chapter's simulator (`wake_sim_v3.py`) is the reference where it and the chapter's prose disagree. R60–R70 were set by the orchestrating session's charter before step 2; the rest are the builders' and the integrators' calls, provisional until ruled.
